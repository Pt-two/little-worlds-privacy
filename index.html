Little Worlds: Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026 · Little Worlds has not been released yet; this page describes the app as it is built today.

In short. Little Worlds is a learning app for young children (letters, numbers and reading in English, Nepali, Hindi and Spanish). It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing about you or your child to us.

What the app keeps, on your device

For each child a grown-up adds, the app keeps:

  • a nickname (please don't use a real name), an animal picture, the look you chose, the languages they learn, and a few settings (such as which hand they write with);
  • how they are getting on: which lessons they finished, their answers in lessons, where they left a lesson, and how long they played each day.

The app uses this information only on your device, to show your child's next step and your child's progress in the grown-ups' area. It is kept in the app's storage on your device, and copied into your device's own backups (such as iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you use them. The developer never receives any of it.

The grown-ups' code

If you set a four-digit code for the grown-ups' area, it is kept as a scrambled value in your device's secure storage (the Keychain), on this device only. It is not synced to other devices, and the developer never receives it.

Purchases

Little Worlds Premium is planned as an annual subscription sold by Apple. Apple handles payment under its own privacy policy; the app only learns whether a subscription is active. Children are never asked to buy anything; purchases are in the grown-ups' area. Purchases are not available yet.

Sounds and voices

Lessons play recordings that come with the app. Little Worlds does not use the microphone for children's lessons. (An earlier, internal version for reviewers can record a grown-up's voice; those recordings stay in the app on the device and in its backups, and are never uploaded. That feature is not part of the app families receive.)

Your choices

  • In the grown-ups' area you can reset a child's progress in one language, or remove a child, which deletes what the app keeps about them on the device.
  • Deleting the app removes its storage from the device. Copies already in your device's backups stay there until those backups are replaced or deleted, and your device may keep the scrambled grown-ups' code in its secure storage after the app is deleted.

Children

Little Worlds is made for children. The developer does not receive children's profiles or learning records: they are processed only on your device, as described above. The app has no chat, no sharing, no advertising and no links a child can follow. Its web and email links (Apple's standard Terms of Use, this page, and the contact address below) are in the grown-ups' area only.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top will change.
